# Red Queen Control 2.0

**Federated Artificial Immune Ecology for AI Trust Domains**

A non-peer-reviewed systems-security concept note exploring whether AI control should shift from primarily containing individual agents toward continuously evolved defensive AI observing population-level behaviour across federated trust domains.

The proposal combines persistent Red Queen attacker/defender co-evolution, heterogeneous observation-specialised defensive AI, ecological risk primitives, cryptographic trust domains, privacy-preserving threat-antigen exchange, and graceful degradation of machine trust across Internet jurisdictions.

## Status

Concept note for discussion, criticism and falsification. This is **not** presented as a solution to alignment, proof of behavioural safety, or production-ready architecture.

## Core hypothesis

As AI becomes an ecosystem of heterogeneous agents, models, humans and services, the primary protected object may need to shift from the behaviour of one model to the health of the surrounding computational ecology.

The proposed architecture has four linked ideas:

1. **Red Queen Foundry** — permanently evolve attacker and defender lineages offline under adversarial pressure, preserve historical failures, and export only frozen, independently qualified defensive snapshots.
2. **Artificial Immune Ecology** — deploy heterogeneous observation-specialised defenders that reason over population-level coordination, propagation, authority accumulation, environmental memory and realised damage rather than relying only on per-agent judgement.
3. **Federated trust domains** — use cryptographic identity, short-lived attestations, local relying-party policy and directional trust peering so organisations and jurisdictions can cooperate without one universal guardian or global trust root.
4. **AI Threat Antigens (ATA)** — exchange signed, privacy-minimised descriptions of discovered ecological threat patterns rather than sharing raw prompts or global surveillance telemetry.

## Research primitives

The note proposes several working concepts for stress-testing:

- **Agency closure** — the transitive set of actors and systems that can be influenced by an AI-originated signal.
- **Protocol closure** — the communication and coordination protocols constructible from all observable or writable resources, including resources never intended for messaging.
- **Environmental memory surface** — external state capable of preserving instructions, capability or coordination across otherwise stateless model instances.
- **Agency reproduction number, R_A** — a research metric for how effectively agency, goals or actionable capability propagate into additional optimisers.
- **Danger field, D(t)** — a dynamic representation of system-level ecological risk rather than one suspicion score per agent.

## What would falsify the thesis?

The thesis should be weakened if ecology-level observers do not generalise beyond their training ecology; Red Queen evolution mainly teaches attackers to defeat the test harness; cross-domain threat-antigen sharing creates little transfer benefit; false positives make useful operation unacceptable; or root/observer compromise creates common-mode failures that local relying parties cannot contain.

The first useful experiment is deliberately small: compare static per-agent monitoring against homogeneous observer ensembles, heterogeneous observer ecologies and Red-Queen-evolved observer ecologies in a controlled multi-agent environment, using a held-out model family and measuring undetected attack success, time-to-detection, false positives, useful task completion and damage before intervention.
